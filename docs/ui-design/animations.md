# Animation plans

1. The stepper in the setup section can be animated to flow the progress bar through the pages. I've made the center element of a stopper spot green so that it
   can expand outwards once the step is completed

   **Built.** A step reached grows its green centre. A step done fills green from the centre, its tick pops in, and the bar fills toward the next step. The rules are in [components.md § Stepper](../../web/src/design/components.md#stepper-stepperdone-ksmqr-active-v0fexw-todo-ugoku), the behaviour in [web-ui.md § Setup wizard](../web-ui.md#setup-wizard).
